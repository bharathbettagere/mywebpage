# LIaN Lab

![img](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bharathbettagere/mywebpage/master/images/Lian_important.png)


LIaN stands for Learning, Inference and NLP. As the name indicates, the main focus of the LIAN lab is to solve learning and inference problems in a networked NLP systems. We focus on using various tools such as probability theory, distributed stochastic optimization, machine learning, information theory, and signal processing to solve some of the existing real-world problems. Currently, the group is focusing on solving problems related to prompting (using mathematical approaches), learning/unlearning using contexts/prompts, federated learning, robust learning etc. My group comprises of bright research students from diverse backgrounds such as mathematics, computer science, and electrical engineering. The following are the research members of my group:

- Sumit Sah (Ph.D. student): Distributed Learning (Generalization and convergence)
- Rahul Kumar Tiwari (JRF): Robust AI
- Avinash (M. Tech student): Prompt design
